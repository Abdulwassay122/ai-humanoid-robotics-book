# Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)

This chapter covers the principles and implementation of effective human-robot interaction (HRI) for humanoid systems, focusing on the unique capabilities and challenges of anthropomorphic robots in social interaction contexts.

## Introduction to Human-Robot Interaction

Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) is a multidisciplinary field that combines robotics, psychology, cognitive science, and human factors engineering to create effective and natural interactions between humans and robots. For humanoid robots, HRI takes on special significance due to their anthropomorphic form and potential for social interaction.
