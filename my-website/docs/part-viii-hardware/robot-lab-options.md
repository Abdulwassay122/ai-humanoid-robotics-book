# Recommended Robot Lab Options (Unitree, Robotis, Hiwonder)

This chapter provides a comprehensive review of commercial humanoid robot platforms suitable for laboratory environments, including Unitree, Robotis, and Hiwonder platforms, comparing their capabilities, limitations, and suitability for different educational and research applications.

## Platform Overview

Commercial humanoid robots provide ready-to-use platforms for education, research, and development in humanoid robotics. These platforms offer various capabilities from basic bipedal locomotion to advanced manipulation and interaction capabilities.

## Unitree Robotics Platforms

### Unitree G1
- **Design Philosophy**: Affordable humanoid robot for research
- **Degrees of Freedom**: 32+ DOF (legs, arms, head)
- **Height**: Approximately 1m tall
- **Weight**: ~35kg
- **Actuators**: Custom high-torque actuators
- **Sensors**: IMU, cameras, force/torque sensors
- **Control**: Real-time control system
- **Software**: ROS 2 integration

### Unitree H1
- **Design Philosophy**: Advanced humanoid for research and applications
- **Degrees of Freedom**: 25+ DOF
- **Height**: 1.3m tall
- **Weight**: ~47kg
- **Payload**: 5kg payload capacity
- **Battery Life**: 2+ hours operation time
- **Locomotion**: Dynamic walking and running
- **Manipulation**: Basic manipulation capabilities

### Unitree Capabilities
- **Locomotion**: Stable bipedal walking
- **Balance Control**: Advanced balance algorithms
- **Development**: Strong ROS integration
- **Safety**: Built-in safety features
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive documentation
- **Support**: Technical support and training

### Unitree Advantages
- **Cost-Effective**: More affordable than many alternatives
- **ROS Integration**: Excellent ROS ecosystem support
- **Development Tools**: Comprehensive development tools
- **Research-Friendly**: Open platform for research
- **Community**: Growing user community

### Unitree Limitations
- **Payload**: Limited payload capacity
- **Speed**: Moderate walking speed
- **Manipulation**: Limited manipulation dexterity
- **Availability**: Supply chain considerations

## Robotis Platforms

### Robotis OP3
- **Design Philosophy**: Educational humanoid platform
- **Degrees of Freedom**: 20 DOF
- **Height**: 72cm tall
- **Weight**: ~5.8kg
- **Actuators**: DYNAMIXEL-X/XL320 actuators
- **Sensors**: Camera, IMU, touch sensors
- **Processing**: Onboard computing capability
- **Software**: Robotis Framework, ROS support

### Robotis OP2
- **Design Philosophy**: Educational platform predecessor
- **Degrees of Future**: 20 DOF
- **Sensors**: Camera, IMU, microphones
- **Processing**: PC-based control
- **Applications**: Educational and research use
- **Community**: Strong educational community

### Robotis Capabilities
- **Education**: Excellent for educational purposes
- **Programming**: Easy programming interface
- **Modularity**: Modular design approach
- **Documentation**: Extensive educational materials
- **Support**: Strong educational support

### Robotis Advantages
- **Educational Focus**: Designed for education
- **Ease of Use**: User-friendly interface
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive documentation
- **Community**: Strong educational community
- **Cost**: Reasonable educational pricing

### Robotis Limitations
- **Size**: Smaller platform limitations
- **Performance**: Limited dynamic performance
- **Manipulation**: Basic manipulation capabilities
- **Research**: Less suitable for advanced research

## Hiwonder Robotics Platforms

### Hiwonder X1
- **Design Philosophy**: Desktop humanoid for education
- **Degrees of Freedom**: 16 DOF
- **Height**: 35cm tall
- **Weight**: ~1.2kg
- **Actuators**: Servo motors with feedback
- **Sensors**: Camera, touch sensors
- **Processing**: Raspberry Pi or Jetson Nano
- **Software**: Custom software with Python API

### Hiwonder Phantom
- **Design Philosophy**: Advanced desktop humanoid
- **Degrees of Freedom**: 32+ DOF
- **Sensors**: Multiple cameras, IMU
- **AI Integration**: Built-in AI capabilities
- **Programming**: Visual and text-based programming

### Hiwonder Capabilities
- **Desktop Platform**: Compact desktop design
- **AI Integration**: Built-in AI and computer vision
- **Programming**: Multiple programming interfaces
- **Education**: Strong educational focus
- **Affordability**: Cost-effective platform

### Hiwonder Advantages
- **Compact Size**: Desktop-friendly design
- **AI Features**: Integrated AI capabilities
- **Programming**: Multiple programming options
- **Price**: Very affordable option
- **Support**: Good technical support

### Hiwonder Limitations
- **Size**: Small size limits applications
- **Payload**: Very limited payload capacity
- **Performance**: Limited dynamic performance
- **Research**: Less suitable for advanced research
