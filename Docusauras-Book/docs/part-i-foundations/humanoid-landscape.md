# Overview of Humanoid Robotics Landscape

This chapter provides a comprehensive overview of the current state of humanoid robotics, including existing platforms, technologies, and research directions.

## Introduction

Humanoid robotics represents one of the most ambitious areas of robotics research, aiming to create machines that resemble and interact with humans in meaningful ways. This field encompasses both research platforms and commercial applications.

## Future Outlook

The humanoid robotics landscape continues to evolve rapidly, with advances in AI, materials science, and control systems promising more capable and useful humanoid robots in the coming decades.

## Summary

The humanoid robotics landscape encompasses a diverse range of platforms and research directions, each contributing to the broader goal of creating useful and capable humanoid systems.
