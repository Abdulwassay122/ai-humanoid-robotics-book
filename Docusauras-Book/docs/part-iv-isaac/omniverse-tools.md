# Using Omniverse Tools for Digital Twins

This chapter covers the use of NVIDIA Omniverse tools for creating and managing digital twins of humanoid robots and their environments, enabling collaborative development and testing.

## Introduction to Omniverse for Digital Twins

NVIDIA Omniverse provides a platform for real-time collaboration and simulation, making it ideal for creating digital twins of complex robotic systems. For humanoid robotics, Omniverse enables the creation of photorealistic, physics-accurate digital replicas that can be used for development, testing, and validation.
